About

Carl's Comics are short stories about something my cockatiel Carl has done. Most of these stories are true, while some obviously aren't. Carl is bossy and aggressive, but when he isn't, he's cute, fluffy, and... still bossy. He often plays board games with us, he's an amazing and ruthless player. No one can win against him. Carl often sits on top of his cage, watching us, copying us, and screaming at us. He's said a few things, but in all of my comics, I'm just guessing what he's thinking and would be saying. Carl loves to rip the erasers off of pencils, and when they're gone, he tries to break the pencil. Carl loves sneaking onto the couch and chewing my hair while I'm distracted. Carl is so cute when he's getting his head scratched, but when I make one wrong move, he tries to murder my finger. Carl is like a crow. He remembers when you do something he doesn't like, and seeks revenge. Not only have I trained Carl, he's trained me.
